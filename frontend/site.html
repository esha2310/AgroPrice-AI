AgroPrice brings a dated WFP food-price snapshot into an approachable workspace. It helps you explore patterns; it cannot explain every price shock or guarantee what a shop will charge.

How well did it forecast?

One-month rolling test. MAE means average absolute error in BDT per unit; lower is better. Results are pooled across eligible markets.

What this does—and does not—tell us

  • Prices include WFP actual and aggregate flags. They are not all individual shop observations.
  • Test months use previously observed prices as lags. This is one-step testing, not a 12-month forecast made at once.
  • Historical data may be revised. This frozen snapshot is not a vintage-safe live backtest.
  • No weather, harvest, transport or policy variables are used. Sudden shocks can be missed.
  • Model feature importance is statistical association, not an explanation of economic causes.
