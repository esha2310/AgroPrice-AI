## Price data

- Provider: United Nations World Food Programme (WFP), distributed through the Humanitarian Data Exchange (HDX).
- Dataset: Bangladesh — Food Prices, `wfp-food-prices-for-bangladesh`.
- Source: https://data.humdata.org/dataset/wfp-food-prices-for-bangladesh
- CSV resource: https://data.humdata.org/dataset/c76eabb7-fdb5-43b7-a5c4-09091bb8acde/resource/966ab7ac-56d6-4dac-8eba-dfe815d59a52/download/wfp_food_prices_bgd.csv
- Licence recorded by the HDX dataset metadata: Creative Commons Attribution for Intergovernmental Organisations, CC BY-IGO 3.0.
- Licence text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/igo/legalcode
- Retrieved: 16 October 2026. Metadata is saved in `source_metadata.json`; the original CSV is `prices_raw.csv`.
- Changes: exact commodity, retail, currency and unit filtering; exclusion of Division pseudo-markets; monthly date normalization; lag features and derived model forecasts. Raw values are not manually corrected. Data attribution does not imply WFP, UN or HDX endorsement of this project or its predictions.

## Original botanical illustrations

`rice-botanical.svg`, `lentils-botanical.svg`, `flour-botanical.svg`, `oil-botanical.svg` and `leaf-botanical.svg` are original code-drawn decorative artwork created for AgroPrice AI. They illustrate crop categories and do not depict a specific reporting market, a measured sample or actual packaging. Their code is covered by the project MIT licence. The wheat artwork decorates the wheat-flour category; the palm artwork illustrates palm oil, not soybean or sunflower oil.
